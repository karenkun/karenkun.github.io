Oakwyn Realty

2021
Property Technology

Oakwyn was growing fast and needed to upgrade their website to meet their business demands. A better experience for property searching, agents listing, agent profiles, agent onboarding and more.

Since their business was expanding to more offices and agents, but their old internal agent portal was not sufficient for their needs. My responsibility was to research and design a new agent portal experience that met their business requirements and optimized the onboarding process for new agents. It was crucial to maintain a consistent and familiar user flow for existing agents while incorporating new system improvements. It was important to keep the agent experience consistent with their established brand but also streamline for optimal usability. My designs reflected their brand and was designed in such a way that easily allowed for future expansion of the internal portal.

Lululemon NYC Re:pair Challenge

2019
Consumer

Web app that allows participants to connect their Strava accounts and run for points with Lululemon’s running event that coincides with the 2019 New York Marathon held annually in November. Participants collect points when they run with other people through a Strava Challenge and then can redeem those points for in-store experiences at the Lululemon pop-up store.

My role was to work with their branding/marketing agency to flesh out a consumer experience that will reach their business goals while being engaging and meaningful for the consumer. This project was a collaborative effort between Good Fortune Creative and the web app development team (Latero Labs).

Timesheet Workforce Management

2018-2020
Financial Technology, Resource Management

Custom designed workforce management system to replace a legacy system that was not meeting the needs of the business. Through user research, testing, and gathering the business requirements from the finance department who sees the largest impact of a better timesheet system.

The new design increased accuracy and efficiency of timesheet data entry on the front lines by providing contextual data validation to handle all the different pay scenarios. Validating and verifying data at the point of entry was one of finance department’s main priorities as clean data was very important for them for streamlining processes.

Extensive usability testing was conducted both on the wireframes in the initial phase and also for the end product on the web as everyone transitioned to use the new system.
